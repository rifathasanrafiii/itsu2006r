# Group presentation recording plan

**Target:** 12-15 minutes total, maximum 250 MB video. Every group member must appear on camera with face visible, share their screen while presenting their section, and state their full name, student ID, and project contribution at the beginning of their own segment.

The group must decide the real speaker allocations. The sequence below is a proposed structure, not a record of completed participation.

| Segment | Time | Visuals | Presenter |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Topic, business problem, objectives | 2 min | Title, customer-support problem, pilot goal | Confirm with group |
| Scope, charter, WBS, schedule | 3 min | In/out scope, WBS, Gantt | Confirm with group |
| Resources, risks, and issues | 3 min | RACI, top risks and responses | Confirm with group |
| Change, communication, quality, GitHub | 3 min | Change workflow, quality gates, real repo evidence | Confirm with group |
| Conclusion and questions | 1-2 min | Acceptance gates and next steps | Confirm with group |

## Recording check

- Use the group's final agreed topic, facts, roles, dates, and report version.
- Show actual GitHub/Jira evidence only if the group has created it.
- Test camera, microphone, screen sharing, readable slides, and file size before recording.
- Confirm each member's visible face, screen share, spoken name, student ID, and role in the final video.
- The group leader uploads the single recorded presentation through the LMS link, not only slides.
